Bag Day Malta 1.1.3

REUSE defaults to the current GPS locality, with saved/manual locality fallback.
Search all localities hides the dropdown; unticking restores the selection.
Public results show their locality and remain newest first.
Every item can include an HTTPS advert URL alongside a phone number.
At least one contact is required; the old contact-mode selector is removed.
Phone numbers accept 8 digits with an optional 356 or +356 prefix.
Phone reveal still requires Google sign-in; advert links remain public.
Admin item review displays both phone and advert URL when present.
API/storage upgrade is required before publishing this website release.
