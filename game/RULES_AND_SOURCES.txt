Sorting Game content review - 24 October 2026 - v1.1.38

Household bags:
https://www.wastecollection.mt/
The existing local waste-guide.js provides the detailed household categories.
New illustrated examples stay within those published categories. Labels
identify empty/clean containers, food removed from packaging, or dirty waste
where that distinction affects the destination. Glass is not added to this
three-bag game. Mixed items are not a catch-all for hazardous waste.

Batteries:
https://greenpak.com.mt/AboutBatteriesforHospice
https://batterybin.mt/
Use only small household battery examples: AA, AAA, button and 9V. No car
batteries, damaged battery packs, vapes or electronic devices in this lesson.
Take household batteries to a dedicated collection bin. Keep button batteries
out of reach of children.
WasteServ also lists household batteries for its Roadshow Truck and Civic
Amenity Sites; the game must not describe a battery bin as the only real route.

Cooking oil and paint:
https://www.wsm.com.mt/en/roadshow
https://www.wsm.com.mt/en/ca-sites
The Roadshow Truck lists cooking oil and paints/solvents among accepted waste.
It has a locality schedule; no nationwide fixed collection day is asserted.
Civic Amenity Sites are also an accepted route. Cooking oil is explicitly
identified so the game does not imply that the Roadshow accepts engine oil.
Oil containers with oil inside are different from empty recyclable containers.

Bulky household furniture:
https://www.wsm.com.mt/en/bulky-waste
https://www.wastecollection.mt/
Use clearly broken or worn-out chairs, sofas, mattresses, tables, wardrobes
and bed frames. Teach booking with the Local Council before putting items out.
Council booking details and item conditions can vary. A Civic Amenity Site is
another option. Usable furniture may instead be passed on for reuse.

Game interpretation:
There are always three visible destinations. A special round has one special
service and two familiar bags, held fixed throughout that minute. Its lesson
explains one item type, and only that type of special item enters that round.
Ordinary items are sampled from the two visible bag categories. Item routes
support more than one accepted visible destination where officially valid.
The game does not require all waste to have exactly one possible real route.
No live collection dates, points, telephone numbers or quantity limits are
hardcoded into the lesson. Official links are provided for further information.

This is household education, not a commercial waste disposal guide. Recheck
operator guidance before extending the catalogue or changing accepted routes.
