Bag Day Malta v1.1.1 - REUSE search and compact sponsors
18 October 2026

REUSE defaults to All localities, with an optional single-locality filter.
Every result identifies its collection locality. Browse and external links
are public; Google sign-in is required to reveal a phone number.

My items appears after sign-in. Owners can mark a listing taken; it leaves
their visible list, frees the listing slot and is omitted from subsequent
public searches. Existing contributor approval and moderation remain.

Home sponsor rows are 44px high and occupy only the space of actual adverts.
Images, text fallback, links and click tracking remain supported.

Deploy the accompanying API update before publishing these public files.
No new database migration or admin replacement is required.
