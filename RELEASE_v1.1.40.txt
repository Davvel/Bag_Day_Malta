BAG DAY MALTA - PORTRAIT GAME, RECEPTACLE GUIDES AND CLOCK
Package 55 | Website v1.1.40 | Based on package 54 / v1.1.39

WHAT CHANGED
1. Game home: modest spacing reductions remove the large gaps. Records,
   level selection, Play and bags use the available portrait screen space.
2. Special introduction: actual images of the two bags and special receptacle
   show the exact left-to-right order used during play. The special receptacle
   has a red border. The order is drawn once and retained when you press Try it.
   Tap a receptacle for its bulleted accepted-items guide. This works before
   and during play; opening a guide during play pauses the timer.
3. Gameplay: a flexible conveyor reserves space for the score, clock and all
   three receptacles, with dynamic viewport height and safe-area padding.
   Active play is portrait only. Landscape entry shows an upright-phone prompt.
   Rotating sideways pauses the round. Turn upright and press Resume yourself.
   Game home, results and reading screens can still scroll if space is limited.
4. No sponsors have been added anywhere in the game area.
6. A compact old-style clock replaces the timer: large seconds, moving hand,
   gentle red pulse only in the last 5 seconds. Reduced-motion users get a
   steady red highlight and no moving hand.

PRESERVED
Levels 1-10 are Classic. Each new round above level 10 has a fresh 60% Classic
chance, with 40% shared equally across Battery / Roadshow / Bulky. You can
still choose Play Classic from a special introduction. Scoring, saved progress,
one-minute duration and realistic lightweight artwork remain in place.

GUIDES
Normal bags retain their existing guides. Special guides cover accepted
categories, beyond just the example in the current lesson. The Roadshow and
bulky lists were checked against official WasteServ pages on 24 October 2026.
The game still uses the selected special lesson plus its two ordinary bags;
the guides explain this distinction. Official links are included in the guides.

2. Open COPY_TO_GITHUB. Copy everything INSIDE it into the root of your
   existing Bag Day WEBSITE repository, replacing matching files and merging
   folders. Do not copy the COPY_TO_GITHUB folder itself.

   repository configuration. This ZIP contains no CNAME.
4. In GitHub Desktop use this commit summary:
   Fit portrait game, add receptacle guides and countdown clock - v1.1.40
5. Click Commit to main, then Push origin. Wait for GitHub Pages to finish.
6. Open Bag Day online, refresh once, close and reopen it. Confirm Settings
   shows v1.1.40. Open Game online once to refresh its separate offline cache.
