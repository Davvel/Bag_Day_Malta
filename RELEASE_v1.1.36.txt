BAG DAY MALTA - MORE ITEMS AND OPTIONAL LEARNING ROUNDS
Package 51 | Website v1.1.36 | Based on package 50 / v1.1.35

WHAT CHANGED
- 86 illustrated sorting items instead of 18: 72 classic household items and
  14 special items, all with static SVG illustrations. No new item animations.
- Classic rounds sample across the larger collection. A shuffled deck avoids
  repeating items before the deck is exhausted and avoids immediate repeats
  across deck boundaries. Round pools grow with level.
- Levels 1-10 always use Organic, Mixed and Recyclables.
- Before EVERY round at level 11 or above (including replay and next level),
  a child-friendly panel introduces one special item type and its destination.
- The player can choose 'Try it' or 'Play Classic'. This choice is offered
  again next time; it does not force or permanently save a special mode.
- Try it uses two ordinary bag columns plus one special destination, kept fixed
  for the whole round. Only suitable items for these columns are generated.
- Special lessons rotate through small batteries, used cooking oil, leftover
  paint and individual bulky furniture items. Each panel explains one type.
- A battery collection bin and two distinct collection-vehicle illustrations
  represent the special destinations. Captions explain asking a grown-up,
  checking the Roadshow stop or booking the Local Council collection.
- Reading the panel is untimed. The one-minute clock starts only after the
  player chooses and the round's pictures are ready. Back/close cancels it.
- The special item appears within the first six draws of a special round.
- Sorting accepts valid overlapping routes. It never teaches that batteries,
  cooking oil or paint belong in ordinary waste bags.
- Tap/select an item, then tap a destination to sort it, or use the existing
  drag/keyboard controls. Tapping a destination with no selected item opens
  its explanation and pauses any running round.
- No new rewards, daily obligations, screen-time incentives or item animations.
  Existing progress, one-minute rounds and settings remain in place.
- The prior Settings splash fix, two-second Home splash and native zoom
  behavior are preserved. Official rule links are in game/RULES_AND_SOURCES.txt.

DEPLOY WEBSITE
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open COPY_TO_GITHUB. Copy everything INSIDE it into the root of your
   existing Bag Day WEBSITE repository, replacing matching files and merging
   folders. Do not copy the COPY_TO_GITHUB folder itself.
3. Keep your existing CNAME, .git folder, live configuration text files and
   repository configuration. This ZIP contains no CNAME.
4. In GitHub Desktop, review the changes and use this commit summary:
   Add illustrated items and optional learning rounds - v1.1.36
5. Click Commit to main, then Push origin. Wait for GitHub Pages to finish.
6. Open Bag Day online and refresh once, close and reopen it. Confirm Settings
   shows v1.1.36. Open Game online once to update its separate offline cache.

PHONE CHECKS
1. Play levels 1-10: only the three ordinary bags should appear. Check the
   clearer static artwork, labels, dragging, keyboard controls if available,
   the optional helper hand and the 60-second end.
2. After passing level 10, select level 11 and Play. Read the battery lesson
   without a timer running. Try it: exactly one battery bin and two bags appear.
3. Return to the game menu and Play level 11 again. A new single-topic lesson
   appears. Choose Play Classic: all three ordinary bags return.
4. Check replay and next level also offer the lesson above level 10.
5. Close the educational panel or press mobile Back: no round should start.
6. During a round, tap a destination without selecting an item. Read its
   explanation, close it and check the paused round resumes correctly.
7. Zoom Home and open Game: verify the existing normal-zoom entry/fallback.
   Check Back restores Home as before, Settings has no splash and Home has
   its minimum two-second splash.
8. After caching the game online, try it offline. New round art should load.
